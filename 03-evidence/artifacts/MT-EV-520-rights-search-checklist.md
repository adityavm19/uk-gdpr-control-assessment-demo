# Synthetic Rights Search Checklist
The workflow records request scope, identity checks where needed, systems searched, search terms, redaction review and secure disclosure. Following DUAA 2025 changes, the template requires a reasoned "reasonable and proportionate search" statement and records any clarification pause.

Advisory observation: 2 of 12 sampled cases contained terse search-rationale wording, but system/source searches and response completeness were corroborated; no material finding was raised.
