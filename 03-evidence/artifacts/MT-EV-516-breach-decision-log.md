# Synthetic Breach Decision Log
Scenario: credential-stuffing and legacy privileged-path compromise led to unauthorised access to 860 customer accounts; contact information for 143 customers was exfiltrated.

Assessment: likely risk to individuals due to account-security implications and targeted follow-on fraud risk. Decision at 15:30 on 12 August 2026: report to the ICO. A separate high-risk assessment was initiated for communications to affected individuals.

The exercise demonstrated a hypothetical submission point 26 hours 30 minutes after awareness; no real notification was made.
