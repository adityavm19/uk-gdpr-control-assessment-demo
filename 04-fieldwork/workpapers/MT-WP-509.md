# Backup restore capability Workpaper

**Workpaper ID:** MT-WP-509  
**Test ID:** MT-TST-509  
**Control / process:** GDPR-CTL-009  
**Evidence:** MT-EV-512;MT-EV-513

## Objective
MTFS can restore availability and access to personal information following an incident within defined recovery objectives.

## Population and sampling
Population: 8 protected backup sets for selected critical systems. Sample: 4 backup sets including core banking, CRM, privacy case management and identity configuration. Rationale: Judgemental sample prioritises highest customer/privacy impact and different recovery technologies.

## Procedure
Reviewed design documentation and source evidence; reconciled the stated population; tested selected attributes; investigated deviations; distinguished design from operating effectiveness.

## Evidence sufficiency
Evidence was considered relevant, timely for the assessment period and corroborated beyond inquiry. Where system-generated evidence was unavailable or incomplete, that limitation is reflected in the conclusion.

## Result
Partial - one critical core-banking restore had no completed annual restore-test evidence.

## Conclusion
Design: Effective. Operating effectiveness: Partially Effective. Exception MT-EXC-503 supports GDPR-FND-003.
