## Objective
Known technical vulnerabilities affecting systems processing personal information are identified and remediated within risk-based timelines.

## Population and sampling
Population: 27 Critical/High vulnerabilities on selected personal-data assets during July 2026. Sample: 27 of 27. Rationale: Full population of highest severity vulnerabilities to avoid understating aged exposure.

## Procedure
Reviewed design documentation and source evidence; reconciled the stated population; tested selected attributes; investigated deviations; distinguished design from operating effectiveness.

## Evidence sufficiency
Evidence was considered relevant, timely for the assessment period and corroborated beyond inquiry. Where system-generated evidence was unavailable or incomplete, that limitation is reflected in the conclusion.

## Result

## Conclusion
Design: Effective. Operating effectiveness: Partially Effective. Exception MT-EXC-505 supports GDPR-FND-005.
