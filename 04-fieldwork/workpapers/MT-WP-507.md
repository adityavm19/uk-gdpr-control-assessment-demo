# Logging & security monitoring Workpaper

**Workpaper ID:** MT-WP-507  
**Test ID:** MT-TST-507  
**Control / process:** GDPR-CTL-007  
**Evidence:** MT-EV-509;MT-EV-510

## Objective
Security-relevant activity from critical personal-data systems is available for timely detection and investigation.

## Population and sampling
Population: 12 critical personal-data/security log sources. Sample: 12 of 12. Rationale: Full population because source coverage can be reconciled directly to the critical-system inventory.

## Procedure
Reviewed design documentation and source evidence; reconciled the stated population; tested selected attributes; investigated deviations; distinguished design from operating effectiveness.

## Evidence sufficiency
Evidence was considered relevant, timely for the assessment period and corroborated beyond inquiry. Where system-generated evidence was unavailable or incomplete, that limitation is reflected in the conclusion.

## Result
Partial - 2 critical sources lacked central ingestion/alert coverage.

## Conclusion
Design: Effective. Operating effectiveness: Partially Effective. Exception MT-EXC-502 supports GDPR-FND-002.
