# Personal-data breach readiness Workpaper

**Workpaper ID:** MT-WP-511  
**Test ID:** MT-TST-511  
**Control / process:** GDPR-CTL-011  
**Evidence:** MT-EV-515;MT-EV-516

## Objective
Potential personal-data breaches are identified, assessed, escalated and capable of notification without undue delay and within applicable statutory timeframes.

## Population and sampling
Population: 1 tabletop plus 9 synthetic privacy incidents in Q2 sample register. Sample: 1 tabletop + 5 incident records. Rationale: Tabletop exercises end-to-end readiness; record sample covers different severity/notification outcomes.

## Procedure
Reviewed design documentation and source evidence; reconciled the stated population; tested selected attributes; investigated deviations; distinguished design from operating effectiveness.

## Evidence sufficiency
Evidence was considered relevant, timely for the assessment period and corroborated beyond inquiry. Where system-generated evidence was unavailable or incomplete, that limitation is reflected in the conclusion.

## Result
Effective - tabletop reached a reasoned reportability decision and prepared notification within 26 hours of awareness.

## Conclusion
Design: Effective. Operating effectiveness: Effective. No material exception identified.
