# Data loss prevention / exfiltration protection Workpaper

**Workpaper ID:** MT-WP-505  
**Test ID:** MT-TST-505  
**Control / process:** GDPR-CTL-005  
**Evidence:** MT-EV-507

## Objective
Controls reduce unauthorised disclosure of restricted personal information through managed endpoints and collaboration services.

## Population and sampling
Population: 12 high-risk DLP policy rules; 37 triggered incidents in assessment period. Sample: 8 incidents plus all 12 policy-rule configurations. Rationale: Configuration full-population plus judgemental incident sample weighted to highest severity.

## Procedure
Reviewed design documentation and source evidence; reconciled the stated population; tested selected attributes; investigated deviations; distinguished design from operating effectiveness.

## Evidence sufficiency
Evidence was considered relevant, timely for the assessment period and corroborated beyond inquiry. Where system-generated evidence was unavailable or incomplete, that limitation is reflected in the conclusion.

## Result
Effective - sampled incidents were triaged and policy configuration matched approved standard.

## Conclusion
Design: Effective. Operating effectiveness: Effective. No material exception identified.
