# Data Subject Rights Process Review

## Scope
Operational review of selected access (Article 15) and erasure (Article 17) workflows. It is a supporting governance exercise, not a legal determination of every right/exemption.

## Population and sample
- Population: 74 synthetic Q2 2026 rights cases.
- Sample: 12 cases — six access and six erasure requests, selected across the quarter and including one clarification pause and one partially fulfilled erasure request.

## Test attributes
1. Request recognised and logged.
2. Identity/authority checks proportionate to the case.
3. Due date calculated and clarification/extension events recorded.
4. Search sources and search rationale documented.
5. Access responses use reasonable and proportionate searches and secure disclosure.
6. Erasure decisions record whether deletion is performed or an exception/continued retention rationale is applied.
7. Response communicated without undue delay and within the applicable workflow deadline.

## Conclusion
All 12 sampled cases were assessed as timely within the documented workflow, including one case with a six-day clarification pause. Two cases used terse wording for the reasonable/proportionate-search rationale; corroborating search evidence was present, so this was recorded as an advisory process-improvement observation rather than a material finding.

Evidence: `MT-EV-519`, `MT-EV-520`.
