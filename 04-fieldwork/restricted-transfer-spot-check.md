# Restricted Transfer Spot Check

## Purpose
Test selected Chapter V governance where personal information may be made available outside the UK. This is not legal advice on transfer validity.

## Selected scenarios
1. `TR-001` — synthetic Microsoft global support access to the United States, using the **UK Addendum** and documented TRA/data protection test.
2. `TR-002` — synthetic Northbridge subprocessor support in the United States, using the **UK IDTA** and documented TRA/data protection test.

## Test attributes
- restricted-transfer decision documented;
- UK safeguard identified (not EU SCCs alone);
- TRA/data protection test completed;
- additional technical/organisational measures recorded where needed;
- transfer record links back to processing/processor context.

## Conclusion
Both selected scenarios passed the spot check. No transfer finding was raised. The portfolio records that ICO guidance still uses the term TRA while current legislation describes the assessment as a data protection test.

Evidence: `MT-EV-521`, `MT-EV-522`.
