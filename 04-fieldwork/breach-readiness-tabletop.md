# Article 33-Style Personal-Data Breach Readiness Tabletop

## Scenario
A synthetic credential-stuffing campaign combined with a weak legacy privileged access path produces unauthorised access to 860 customer accounts and confirmed exfiltration of contact details for 143 customers. No real event or customer exists.

## Objectives
- establish the operational point of awareness;
- contain and investigate;
- bring the DPO/privacy team into incident command early;
- assess likelihood of risk to individuals and document the notification decision;
- produce minimum notification content with known facts and phased-update capability;
- consider whether the higher Article 34 communication threshold is met;
- preserve a breach record even if notification were ultimately not required.

## Result
The simulated awareness point was 09:05 on 12 August 2026. The DPO was engaged at 09:42, the reportability decision was documented at 15:30, and the hypothetical ICO submission package was ready at 11:35 on 13 August — **26 hours 30 minutes after awareness**. The exercise therefore demonstrated operational capacity to act inside the Article 33 72-hour outer window where notification is required.
