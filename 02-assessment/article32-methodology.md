# Article 32 Assessment Methodology

## Assessment lens
The matrix evaluates selected **control outcomes**, not legal compliance with Article 32 as a whole. Each control is anchored to personal-data risk and tested for both design and operating effectiveness where evidence permits.

## Design effectiveness
- **Effective:** the control as designed is capable of meeting the stated objective for the scoped risk.
- **Partially Effective:** design gaps or uncontrolled exceptions reduce reliability.
- **Ineffective:** design is materially incapable of meeting the objective.

## Operating effectiveness
The workpaper tests whether the control operated consistently over the assessment period, using defined population/sample and evidence sufficiency criteria.

## Rating
Findings use the portfolio 5x5 likelihood × impact model inferred from the canonical enterprise risk register: 1-4 Low, 5-9 Moderate, 10-16 High, 17-25 Critical. Planned remediation does not reduce the historical finding rating.

## Result
12 control outcomes assessed: 7 Effective and 5 Partially Effective. No control was rated wholly Ineffective. The five partial outcomes are expressed as findings only where evidence demonstrated a material condition.
