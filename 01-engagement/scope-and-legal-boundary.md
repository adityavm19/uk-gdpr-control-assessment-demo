# UK GDPR Security Control Assessment — Scope & Legal Boundary

## Engagement type
**Security and technical/organisational control assurance supporting UK GDPR compliance.** This is not a legal compliance determination, legal advice, an ICO audit, a regulatory assurance engagement, or certification.

## Assessed entity
- `MT-ENT-002` — Meridian Trust Bank plc (fictional UK operating bank/controller).
- Assessment period: 1 April 2026 to 31 July 2026.
- Fieldwork window: 3 August 2026 to 21 August 2026.
- Report date: 1 September 2026.

## Objectives
1. Assess whether selected technical and organisational security measures are designed and operating in a manner proportionate to risks to personal information, with Article 32 as the principal assurance lens.
2. Test whether selected supporting privacy/accountability processes provide usable evidence of control operation: ROPA, personal-data breach escalation, rights handling, processor security oversight and restricted-transfer governance.
3. Reuse canonical MTFS risks, systems, evidence concepts and underlying issue IDs so that the same technical condition is not relabelled merely because it is viewed through UK GDPR.

## Risk-based in-scope areas
- Identity and privileged access (`MT-IAM-001`, `MT-RSK-001`).
- Encryption and data protection for customer systems (`MT-RSK-007`).
- Vulnerability management (`MT-VUL-001`, `MT-RSK-003`).
- Security logging and monitoring (`MT-LOG-001`, `MT-RSK-004`).
- Availability, backup and restore testing (`MT-RES-001`, `MT-RSK-005`).
- Security-control effectiveness testing and incident response (`MT-RSK-009`).
- Processor security and ongoing assurance (`MT-TPR-001`, `MT-RSK-006`).
- ROPA and selected rights/transfer governance supporting accountability.

## Explicit exclusions
- Lawful basis determinations, privacy notices, PECR/direct marketing, employment-law issues, DPIA legal sufficiency, automated decision-making legality and full retention-law analysis.
- Full Article-by-Article UK GDPR compliance review.
- EEA GDPR/DORA compliance of `MT-ENT-003`, except where a shared group system appears as a technical dependency.
- Penetration testing, red-team testing or source-code review.
- Financial-statement ITGC or SAP control testing.

## Assessment criteria
- UK GDPR Article 5(1)(f) and Article 5(2) as security/accountability context.
- UK GDPR Article 32 for security of processing.
- UK GDPR Article 33 for breach notification readiness.
- UK GDPR Articles 15 and 17 for selected rights-process workflow review.
- UK GDPR Articles 28(1) and 28(3) for processor selection/contract security assurance.
- UK GDPR Chapter V / Article 46 concepts for selected restricted-transfer spot checks.
- Current ICO guidance and Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 changes where material.

## Overall limitation
The work provides a portfolio assurance conclusion over selected controls and evidence for a fictional environment. It does not establish legal compliance with the UK GDPR as a whole.
