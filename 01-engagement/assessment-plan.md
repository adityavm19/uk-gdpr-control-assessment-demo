# Assessment Plan

| Workstream | Risk rationale | Method | Planned output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Processing/data context | Article 32 appropriateness depends on nature, scope, context and purpose of processing | ROPA/data-flow review | ROPA extract + selected processing inventory |
| Article 32 measures | Highest security exposure in a digital bank | Design + operating-effectiveness testing | Control matrix + workpapers |
| Breach readiness | Failure to assess/escalate within statutory window amplifies harm | Tabletop + evidence trail | Scenario timeline and decision log |
| Rights handling | Operational process supports accountability and secure disclosure/deletion | Sample test | SAR/erasure workpaper |
| Processor security | Material processors hold customer information | Due diligence/contract sample | Processor review |
| Restricted transfers | International access/support may create Chapter V obligations | Mechanism + TRA/data-protection-test spot check | Transfer register extract |

## Sampling approach
Sampling is risk-directed rather than statistical. Full-population testing is used where the population is small and security-critical (privileged identities and critical log sources). Judgemental samples target higher-risk systems, material processors, and a mix of request types/time periods. Each workpaper documents its population, sample, rationale and limitations.

## Evidence sufficiency
Inquiry is used for process understanding but not as sole evidence of operating effectiveness. Conclusions require corroborating system exports, tickets, reports, contracts, test records or generated synthetic artifacts.
