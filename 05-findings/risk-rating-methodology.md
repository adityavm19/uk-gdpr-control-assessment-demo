# Finding Risk Rating Methodology

Likelihood and impact are scored 1-5. Score = likelihood × impact. Thresholds are deterministic and aligned to the pattern in the canonical enterprise risk register:

| Score | Rating |
|---:|---|
| 1-4 | Low |
| 5-9 | Moderate |
| 10-16 | High |
| 17-25 | Critical |

Historical finding ratings are not reduced because remediation is planned. A changed residual exposure is recorded only after evidence-backed remediation/retest.
